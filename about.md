I’m a software engineer at Samsung Electronics, currently on academic leave pursuing an M.S. in Computer Science at the University of Southern California.

My work on Samsung Notes spans Android product development, application performance, cross-device collaboration, and applied AI/ML. I’ve built LLM-based document-generation pipelines, retrieval-augmented document discovery, multimodal API integrations, and a bilingual DistilBERT classifier. I’ve also implemented collaborative editing across Android and Windows using C++ and JNI, and improved startup performance and memory behavior.

My toolkit includes C++, Java, Kotlin, and Python, alongside PyTorch, TensorFlow, and ONNX Runtime. I’m interested in the engineering work that turns AI capabilities into reliable, useful product features.

I enjoy taking an idea from user flows and system behavior through implementation, testing, and integration. I’m interested in opportunities across software engineering, applied AI/ML, and systems development.
